Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How Snehal Business Hub handles your communication, inquiry details, and personal data.

1. Introduction & Scope

Snehal Business Hub operates as an independent product-promotion and enquiry platform associated with Wealth Craft Era Lifestyle Private Limited. We prioritize ethical communication, consumer trust, and personal data transparency. This Privacy Policy details the limited information collected when you explore our website or contact us through WhatsApp and our contact form.

2. Information We Collect

Because this website is operated as a static platform, we do not store customer records or financial data on a centralized server database. We only receive information that you voluntarily choose to share with us:

  • Contact Details: Your full name, Indian mobile number, and email address when submitted via our enquiry form.
  • Inquiry Content: Specific product names, category interests, or questions regarding distributor pricing and the promoter network.
  • Direct Messages: Communication initiated by you via WhatsApp (+91 76662 19432) or telephone calls.

3. How We Use Your Information

Any details provided by visitors are used strictly to:

  • Respond directly to your inquiries regarding product availability, specifications, directions for use, and distributor pricing.
  • Provide transparent information regarding the business promoter network, official registration guidelines, and compensation structures associated with Wealth Craft Era Lifestyle Private Limited.
  • Follow up on pending questions or product delivery queries that you have explicitly requested.

4. Zero Third-Party Data Selling or Sharing

We firmly respect your privacy:

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details to external marketing agencies or data aggregators.
  • We do not enroll visitors into automated robocall lists or unsolicited spam campaigns.
  • Your communication is retained strictly for one-to-one correspondence between you and our authorized representatives.

5. Third-Party Links & WhatsApp Communication

Our website provides direct interactive links to WhatsApp (operated by Meta Platforms, Inc.). When you initiate a chat through WhatsApp, your interactions are governed by WhatsApp’s own Privacy Policy and end-to-end encryption standards. We encourage you to review their respective privacy practices when using their messaging platform.

6. Data Security & Contact Form Notice

Our inquiry form operates on client-side validation without storing entries in an unencrypted web database. When you submit the form, it pre-fills a message that opens directly in your official WhatsApp application, giving you complete visibility and control over what is transmitted.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
